# How to Rank #1 in Jamshedpur (Strategy Guide)

Ranking #1 on Google is a competition. To beat other AC repair shops in Jamshedpur, you need to do these 3 things consistently.

## 1. Google Business Profile (The "Map Pack")
When someone searches "AC Repair near me", Google shows a **Map** with 3 top businesses first. This is where you want to be.
*   **Action**: Get regular reviews. Every time you fix an AC, ask the customer: *"Sir/Ma'am, if you liked our service, please give us a 5-star rating on Google Maps."*
*   **Action**: Upload photos of your work (e.g., "Chiller repair at Zenith Forge") to your Google Profile weekly.

## 2. Keywords in Content (We just did this!)
I have updated your website's main headline (H1) to say **"Jamshedpur's Expert AC & Chiller Service"** instead of generic text.
*   This tells Google exactly *where* and *what* you do.
*   **Action**: When you list services, mention locations. Example: "AC Repair in Adityapur", "Fridge Repair in Sakchi".

## 3. Backlinks (Votes of Confidence)
If other Jamshedpur websites link to you, Google thinks you are important.
*   **Action**: List your business on **JustDial**, **Sulekha**, and **IndiaMART**.
*   **Action**: Since you work with clients like **Zenith Forge** and **Rungta Steel**, ask if they can put your logo or link on their "Vendors" or "Partners" page.
