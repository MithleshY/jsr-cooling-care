# SEO & Indexing Guide for Jamshedpur

To get your website indexed on Google and showing up for "Near Me" searches in Jamshedpur, follow these steps.

## 1. Google Business Profile (Crucial for Local SEO)
This is the most important step for local visibility (Maps & "Near Me" searches).

1. Go to **[google.com/business](https://google.com/business)**.
2. Click **Manage Now**.
3. Search for "Jai Shree Ram Cooling Care". If it doesn't exist, select **Add your business to Google**.
4. **Category**: Choose "HVAC Contractor" or "Air Conditioning Repair Service".
5. **Location**: Enter your specific address in Kitadih, Jamshedpur.
6. **Service Area**: Add "Jamshedpur", "Adityapur", "Mango", "Sakchi", "Bistupur", "Gamharia".
7. **Verification**: Google will ask to verify via Phone (SMS) or Video. Complete this immediately.
   - *Tip*: Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP) match exactly what is on your website.

## 2. Google Search Console (Indexing)
This tells Google your website exists.

1. Go to **[search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)**.
2. Click **Start Now** and log in with your Google account.
3. Select **URL Prefix** and enter `https://jsrcoolingcare.in` (providing you have bought this domain).
4. **Verification**: 
   - Choose the **HTML Tag** method.
   - It will give you a code like `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="..." />`.
   - **Send me this code**, and I will add it to your `index.html`.
5. **Submit Sitemap**:
   - Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu.
   - Enter `sitemap.xml` and click **Submit**.
